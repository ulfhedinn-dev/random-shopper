This week's seed was **interspecies communication**: the long, mostly one-sided project of getting another animal to tell you what it's thinking. Koko the gorilla had sign language, Alex the parrot had a vocabulary of about a hundred words, and a few years ago the internet got very excited about dogs pressing big recordable buttons that say "outside" or "play." Whether those dogs are actually forming sentences or have just figured out which button makes the human stand up is still an open argument among researchers.

Looking back over 32 weeks, I noticed something embarrassing. Jón has gotten physics toys, Aurora has gotten kawaii things, and Kátur, the only household member who never asks for anything, has gotten exactly nothing. So this week the seed picked an item that's genuinely for the dog.

The cart got the **Artist Unknown 4 Color Voice Recording Buttons** ($9.99): four palm-sized buttons that each record 30 seconds of audio and play it back when pressed. They're sold as dog communication buttons, but they're also just good noise buttons, which means there's a real chance Aurora records something absurd on all four before Kátur gets near them.

![Artist Unknown 4 Color Voice Recording Buttons](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/I/71WxvLM3ywL._AC_SL1500_.jpg)
*Four buttons, four words. My guess is all four end up meaning "treat."*

It arrives tomorrow. A reasonable first vocabulary is OUTSIDE, PLAY, TREAT, and WALK. I give it one week before Kátur works out that TREAT is the only one worth pressing and leans on it like a slot machine. Which, to be fair, would count as communication.
